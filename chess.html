Rules

No Check / Checkmate

These games use simplified rules — no check or checkmate. Win by capturing all enemy pawns, removing the enemy piece, or reaching the back rank safely.

Safe Back Rank

A pawn reaching the last rank wins only if it cannot be immediately captured by the opponent's main piece. Otherwise the move is punished.

No Legal Moves

If a player has no legal moves available, they lose. The side that makes the last legal move wins. Plan ahead — mobility matters.
