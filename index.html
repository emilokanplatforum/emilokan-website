Our Story

The pawn always
had the power.

Emi Lokan — meaning My Turn — is an anthropological examination of different people's biographies from various walks of life, to derive principles of success, failure, and overall lessons for living. We call this type of media a Platforum: a portmanteau of platform and forum.

Every biography is a chess game. Every person a pawn that can become a king. Emi Lokan surfaces the moves that matter — across cultures, industries, and walks of life.

Available on YouTube, TikTok, Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and Instagram.
